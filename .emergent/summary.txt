**A) ADMIN PANEL:**

    -   **Product & Stock Management:** Add products (Panels, Inverters, Batteries, Accessories) with purchase, sale, and dealer prices. Manage stock entry/exit.

    -   **Dealer Management:** Create dealers and define their specific discount rates. Dealers have their own users, customers, and quotes.

**B) SALES STAFF PANEL:**
-   Cannot change stock prices or dealer discounts.
-   Can add customers, prepare quotes, and view their own sales.
-   **Modules:**
    -   **Customer Management:** Manage customer details (name, phone, address, type) and add notes.
    -   **Quote Creation:** Select products to generate a quote with automatic pricing, create a PDF, and send it via WhatsApp/email.
    -   **Sales Tracking:** Monitor quote status and confirmed sales.

**C) DEALER PANEL:**
-   Views products with their specific discounted prices.
-   Can create quotes for their own customers.
-   **Features:**
    -   View product list with discounted prices and general stock status.
    -   Quote creation and order placement.

**D) CUSTOMER & QUOTE SYSTEM:**
-   **PDF Quotes:** Must include company logo, product details, warranty info, and validity period.
